As I celebrated my 30th birthday this weekend, I was curious how many more pieces of media and literature I can consume in a lifetime, considering all things go well.

“Rough math: at 30, you’ve got something like 50 more years, and maybe 45 of them are solid movie-watching years. From there it’s just a rate question.

One a month → about 550
One a week → about 2,300
Two a week → about 4,700
One a day → about 16,400”

I now know two things:

I need to hit at least 2 movies a week to feel okay about myself
Claude thinks I’m going to die around 80

Years ago, I was scouring the streaming sites to find the movie called Girl on the Motorcycle, starring Marianne Faithfull and Alain Delon. An image of her in a leather jumpsuit was burned into my brain, and it was time to see what it was all about. The completely 60s, overtly sexy film with bad special effects was love at first sight. Prior to clicking on the play, I noticed the one sentence synopsis. I liked that each word was accounted for. I thought of all the copywriting interns, marketing monkeys, public relations wizards, and the in house entertainment people who strung together this series of words in an attempt to sell the hell out of the movie. And I wondered what the screenwriter, who I imagine had no part in this side of production, thought of it. Condensing a two hour story into two sentences is surely no easy task. I’d bet that many movie synopsis are left to the agency of AI today. I thought of some of my favorite films and series, and felt it was important to jot down the stories that I carry with me today.

This list will be continuously updated.
